About A kids India

Creating Playful Learning Environments Across India

A kids India is a New Delhi-based manufacturer and supplier of kindergarten furniture, playground equipment, and sports gear. We serve schools, daycare centres, preschools, and families across India who believe that well-designed spaces help children learn, move, and grow with confidence.

Our product range brings together three interconnected categories under one roof: Indoor — smart, stackable classroom furniture and soft play essentials; Outdoor — multi-play stations, adventure structures, and safety surfacing; and Shreem Sports — professional-grade sports gear for the next generation of athletes.

Whether you are equipping a new preschool, upgrading an existing playground, or stocking a school sports programme, we make it simple to explore products, request a quote, and get the right guidance for your project.
